# 1. Vision

Wisdum is not a Knowledge Management System.

Wisdum is not a Note Taking App.

Wisdum is not another ChatGPT wrapper.

Wisdum is an AI Operating System.

Its purpose is to continuously understand everything a person or organization knows, identify opportunities hidden inside that knowledge, and proactively help create valuable outputs.

Knowledge is not the product.

Reasoning is the product.

Opportunities are the product.

Creation is the product.

---

# 2. Mission

Every professional creates knowledge.

Very little of it becomes leverage.

Code is forgotten.

Research is forgotten.

Ideas disappear.

Architecture discussions disappear.

Slack conversations disappear.

Git history disappears.

Meeting notes disappear.

Claude conversations disappear.

The mission of Wisdum is to continuously transform scattered knowledge into reusable intelligence.

---

# 3. The Problem

Today's AI works like this:

Human
↓

Prompt

↓

AI Response

↓

Done

This is fundamentally reactive.

The user must remember:

- what they learned
- what they built
- what they researched
- what they wanted to write

AI does not.

Wisdum reverses this.

Knowledge is continuously collected.

AI continuously reasons.

The user receives opportunities instead of starting from blank pages.

---

# 4. Core Philosophy

The user should never ask:

"What should I write?"

Instead Wisdum should say:

"Here are the ten highest-value things you should publish this week."

---

# 20. Engineering Principles

Software exists to serve the product.

If architecture makes the product harder to build:

Architecture changes.

The product does not.

Every engineering decision must answer:

"Does this improve Wisdum's ability to discover opportunities?"

If the answer is no,

it probably doesn't belong in the core platform.

---

# 21. Definition of Wisdum

Wisdum is an AI Operating System that continuously transforms everything a person or organization knows into opportunities to create, publish, and compound knowledge.

Everything else is implementation.
